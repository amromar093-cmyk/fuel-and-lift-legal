2. Services that receive information

  • Supabase (database, accounts and server functions, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany): stores your account, backup, friends and shared products.
  • Google (Gemini AI): when you analyse a meal photo, the photo and your optional note are sent through our server to Google’s Gemini API to estimate the meal. Google processes it under its API terms. If you enter your own OpenAI key instead, the photo goes directly from your phone to OpenAI.
  • Open Food Facts (open database of packaged foods): when you scan a barcode or search packaged foods, the barcode or search words and your country are sent to Open Food Facts to find the product. No account details are sent.
  • Apple: App Store purchases, TestFlight, and push or local notifications. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to these.

We share information only with these services and only to run the features you use, or if the law requires it.

3. Camera, photos and notifications

The camera is used only to scan barcodes and take meal photos. Photo library access is used only when you choose a profile picture or a meal photo. Notifications are used only for the reminders you turn on. You can turn each permission off in iPhone Settings.

4. How long we keep it

Your account and backup stay until you delete your account. You can delete your account at any time inside the app (Profile → Delete account). That permanently deletes your account, backup, friends, cheers and usage records from our servers. Products you added to the shared product list stay, but with your account removed from them, because they belong to everyone. Data stored on your phone stays until you delete the app.

5. Your rights and choices

You can see and edit your data in the app, delete your account in the app, or write to us at amromar093@gmail.com to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We will answer within 30 days.

6. Legal basis (EU/UK users)

We process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract), because you gave consent for health data and photo analysis (you can withdraw it by stopping photo analysis or deleting your account), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and preventing abuse.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), accounts are protected by authentication, and access rules limit each person to their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

The app is not for children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK). We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it. The app’s weight and calorie tools can be unsuitable for young people; please see the health notice in our Terms.

9. International transfers

Our servers are in Germany, and the AI service is operated by Google, which may process data in other countries. Where required, these providers use recognised safeguards for transfers.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
